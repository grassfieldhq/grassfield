# grassfield-ui spec · shell-engineer · M1 (spike S1)

## macOS reference behavior (from design/reference/, described in words — no Apple images committed)
The reference analysis is `docs/testing/f1-reference-gaps.md` (our own measurements from private macOS 26 captures; owner sign-off F1 pending). Every token in `ui/tokens/tokens.json` carries a `source` note that points at that analysis, a spec section, an ADR or a test; `gen-tokens.py` fails when one does not (GF-095): a source must cite something that exists, in one of the forms `docs/**.md § <section>` (a heading of that file; the F1 analysis may list several), `master-prompt § <n.n>`, `ADR-nnnn section <n>`, a `docs/` or `tests/` path, or be a declared estimate with its reason (`estimate: a pressed light darkens by this much`); `tests/ci/token_sources.py` renames one pointer of each kind and expects the lint to fail. Where the note says "estimate", no reference value exists yet.

## Purpose and non-goals
Design system shared by the shell and (later) native apps: tokens, frosted material, appearance state and the M1 component set (Icon, PopupMenu/MenuItem/MenuSeparator, Slider, Toggle, Button, Tooltip). Non-goals: any Quickshell or Hyprland import, business logic.

## Functional requirements
1. `ui/tokens/tokens.json` is the single source of truth; `ui/tools/gen-tokens.py` generates `Tokens.qml`; `--check` exits 1 when stale.
2. `Appearance` exposes `dark` (default true), `transparency` (default 0.25), `reduceTransparency` (forces effective transparency 0), `reduceMotion` (durations capped at `motionReducedMax` = 100 ms).
3. `Material` tint alpha = `Appearance.tintAlpha(t)`: `1 - t * (1 - tintAlphaMin)` (0.85 at the default t = 0.25), clamped to `tintAlphaClamp` up to `tintClampUntil` (A2: 0.82 up to t = 0.6), then linear to `tintAlphaMin` at t = 1; adds the glass edge (ADR-0024: one device pixel of darker outer edge, `borderColor`/`borderOpacity`, dark in both appearances, and one device pixel of brighter inner specular rim, `highlightOpacity`, half strength all round and stronger toward the light, the top left (ADR-0024 section 2.5, ADR-0039 step 22): full strength round the top-left corner, fading back to half along the straight parts of the top and left edges; no gradient band; Increase Contrast raises the outer edge in both appearances, `material.borderOpacity` 0.5 and `material.edgeOpacityDark` 0.96) and a gaussian shadow (`RectangularShadow`, QtQuick.Effects 6.9+, verified in Qt 6.11.2) masked so it never shows under the glass.
3b. Adaptive glass (ADR-0024 section 2, G-1 to G-3; owner decision 2026-10-26): a `Material` with a `glassClass` ("menu": PopupMenu, VolumeMenu, the Dock stack, dialogs, Search; "notification": banners, the column, Notification Center; "controls": Quick Settings modules, the OSD, the Stages badges; "apps": the Apps panel; "gallery": the Overview's Desktops band, the widget gallery; "widgets": the desktop's and the Notification Center's widgets; "menu" also takes every other text surface: tooltips, the switcher, the Overview's window label, the About This Wallpaper card, Preview's Info popover, the shared `Sheet`) takes `Appearance.glassAlpha(class, t, output)` (`GlassTint.js`): the class target (`glass.*`: 0.65, 0.60, 0.15, 0.55, 0.15, 0.92 at the default slider; 1 at t = 0, falling to 0 at t = 1) raised to the smallest alpha that keeps `text` and `textSecondary` at 4.5:1 over the backdrop, with and without the fill overlay. The backdrop is `Appearance.backdrops[output]` (`{lumMin, lumMax}`, fed by the shell's `Backdrop` service only while the output shows the bare desktop: no window on its active workspace, `Compositor.bareOutputs`, over a wallpaper of known luminance: a colour exactly, the artwork's whole palette, a still picture's 5th-95th percentile from the helper's `image` statistics) plus `glass.contrastMargin`; elsewhere the worst case (black to white: 0.78 light, 0.82 dark at the default slider, where the one tint was 0.85). Each end of the range is bounded by its grey and its saturated colours (`GlassTint.backdropsOf`). Tests: `tst_glasstint.qml`; A2 `test_glass_classes_hold_a2_over_any_backdrop` and `..._over_a_known_backdrop`. The menu bar band is the "menu" class too, with its tone adapted to the strip under it (`Appearance.barBand`, `GlassTint.bandAlpha`: judged at rest, under the open title capsule and dimmed; docs/specs/menubar.md item 0b). Without a class the one tint applies (the Dock shelf, which sets its own, and the security dialogs, outside ADR-0039). A piece of glass outside a surface's shape takes the surface's own tint as drawn (`Material.fillColor`, `PopupMenu.glassFill`): the tails of the Dock menu, the stack popup and the tooltip. The close capsules of banners and cards, which no blur lies under, keep their near-opaque `notification.closeTintOpacity` through `Appearance.fixedGlassAlpha`: opaque under Reduce Transparency, frosted by Increase Contrast (ADR-0039 step 22). Per surface (lot 6): the wallpaper on each output is also read as a luminance grid (`glass.gridColumns` x `gridRows`, 16 x 10; the shown wallpaper grabbed at `gridSample` px per cell and read back cell by cell when it becomes dominant, never per frame; none for a colour, a video or the dev image), `Backdrop.grids`, passed with the range; a surface's range is then the cells under its area grown by `glass.backdropPad` (24, the blur's reach; `GlassTint.gridRange`). The area is `Material.backdropRect` when the host sets it (the banner stack, whose window is anchored top-right; the Notifications column at rest, for cards that scroll), else measured by the Material when its window covers the output (Quick Settings, menus, Search, Apps), else unknown (the whole output). Over the default artwork the top-right area (Quick Settings, the column, the banners) reads 0.02-0.35 where the whole output is 0.02-0.88 (the sun). Increase Contrast frosts every glass surface to at least `glass.contrastFrost` 0.9 (G-8), with the stronger rim and border tokens; Reduce Transparency makes it opaque. The Dock is clear glass (G-7, `dock.tintAlpha*` 0.05) with a haloed running dot (docs/specs/dock.md). Not yet: windows behind a surface on a non-bare desktop (compositor side); the dark mid-grey glass (G-6): with today's `textSecondary` (#DCDCE4) and the 0.14 fill overlay, a lifted grey tint (#4A4C52 to #5E6066) fails A2 over any backdrop brighter than about 0.03 (computed with the A2 model), so it needs a brighter dark secondary text or a lighter fill first (owner decision); the blur's vibrancy (G-4) needs the nested session to calibrate.
4. `Material` never blurs. The window declares `BackgroundEffect.blurRegion: Region { item: material; radius: material.radius }` when `material.blurActive`.
5. Font family Inter, fallback Noto Sans (first installed wins). Text of 20 px and more uses Inter Display (`typography.displayFamily`, `displayFrom` 20; ADR-0003 O1, T-1 of ADR-0024's plan), through `Appearance.fontFamilyFor(px)` and with no added tracking (`Appearance.trackingFor`: the display cut carries its own spacing); Inter when the display cut is not installed. Used by the Apps header and the Search field, and by the widgets' large figures (`WidgetLabel.size`). The lock screen's clock and date (shell/security) keep Inter until a security review covers the change.
6. `Icon { name, color, size }` draws `branding/icons/symbolic/<name>.svg` (monochrome black) recolored with `MultiEffect` colorization (brightness 1 + colorization 1). `directory` is overridable for installed layouts.
6a. `TintedIcon { source, size, mode?, tint?, oversample? }` draws an application or file icon (an image URL: a theme icon path, `image://icon/<name>`, a file URL) in the user's icon appearance mode, see "Icon appearance modes". Never for symbolic UI glyphs (`Icon` keeps those) or content (thumbnails, notification pictures).
7. `PopupMenu { model }` with `model: [{id, text, name?, shortcut?, iconName?, enabled?, checked?, destructive?, separator?, submenu?, alternate?}]`; signals `activated(item)` and `closeRequested()`. Width: never wider than `menu.maxWidth` (a longer row is shortened in its middle, UI audit 2026-11-05 U10); a row's `name` (an item's name put in place of the label's `%1`: Compress “%1”, Glance at “%1”) is drawn shortened in its middle to `menu.nameMaxWidth`, its start, its extension and the words around it kept, so an item menu keeps its usual width whatever the name (macOS Finder; owner report 2026-11-07), while its accessible name keeps the whole name. A label sent to the shell's menu bar (an app's global menu) is shortened the same way by the app before it is sent (Files: `BrowserWindow.menuName`). Rows: 24 px; highlight on hover and keyboard selection in `colors.selection` with `colors.selectionText` (white on the accent in both appearances), concentric with the menu (radius `radius.menu` − `menu.padding`); labels `menu.itemPadding` inside the row, after the check column (`menu.checkInset`) in a section that holds a checkable row (a model entry with `checked`), an icon shifting only its own row (macOS 27); whole-pixel menu width; right-aligned shortcut in the device's style (`MenuShortcut.label` with `Appearance.shortcutStyle`: the text on a PC, the macOS symbols on a Mac; docs/specs/settings.md, Keyboard), chevron for submenus, disabled at `material.disabledOpacity`. Keys: Up/Down/Home/End, Return/Space, Right (open submenu), Left (close), Esc. The navigation model is `MenuNav.js` (pure).
8. `Slider` (value, from, to, stepSize, `moved(value)`; arrow/Home/End keys), `Toggle` (checked, `toggled(checked)`; Space/Return; `large`: the 44 x 20 switch of a pane's header row; on, its track, a `Switch`'s and a `Slider`'s fill take the selection colour, the fill of a checked box and a default button, and the white knob is outlined in the fill's mark colour where it holds under `control.knobMinContrast` 3:1 there, the yellow and green fills: ADR-0039 step 11), `SegmentedControl` (`fill`: equal segments across its width; `prominent`: the selected segment in the selection colour), `Button` (primary/destructive; `push`: the macOS 27 24-tall capsule, as wide as its label plus `control.pushPadding` on each side; `sheet`: the same size with a `control.sheetButtonRadius` corner, a sheet's buttons), `Tooltip` (text, shown, delay). All have `Accessible` role/name, one keyboard focus ring (3 px, `control.focusOutline`, opaque, `Appearance.focusRingColor`: the accent, or the text colour where the accent holds under 3:1 against the material, i.e. the light appearance's yellow; `tst_accent.qml` checks 3:1 for every accent in both appearances; password fields wear this shared ring outside the field, the lock's field none, UI audit 2026-11-05 U23; only the polkit identity rows keep `control.focusRing` 2 px), Reduce Motion aware. Increase Contrast and Text Size: tokens with an `increaseContrast` value or `textScale: true` in `tokens.json` are generated as bindings to `Appearance.increaseContrast` / `Appearance.textScale` (`ui/tools/gen-tokens.py`).

## Icon appearance modes (ADR-0014 phase 1, provisional)
Every Grassfield surface that shows an app or file icon draws it through `TintedIcon`: Dock (tiles and minimized badge), Apps panel, Search results, desktop icons, Overview (Space tile icons, window name pill), the About and Force Quit panels, and the file-type icons of Grassfield Files (list, icon, column, gallery views and Get Info). The mode is `Appearance.iconMode`, fed from `icons.mode` by the shell (`Settings.iconMode`) and by Files and Settings (they read `grassfield.json` and follow it live, no restart). Not tinted, as in macOS: symbolic glyphs (`branding/icons/symbolic`), the Files sidebar and toolbar glyphs, file thumbnails, notification pictures, drag images. The macOS 26 model, what each mode does to a theme icon (every icon sits on a system plate and its artwork is recoloured by a fixed transform, as macOS does for icons that are not made for the mode; numbers are the `iconMode` token group):
- `default`: the icon as is. No effect item exists (`Loader.active` is false): no extra render pass, no cost.
- `dark` (macOS 26 Dark): the system plate is graphite (`darkPlateLuma` 0.13) (no edge highlight, see step 3 below) and the artwork sits on it scaled to `sysGlyphScale`, a little desaturated (`darkArtSaturation`) and mapped affinely in linear light from `darkArtLift` (black: it separates a little from the graphite) to `darkArtScale` (white); colour identity kept.
- `clear` (macOS 26 Clear): the plate is translucent glass (white at `clearPlateAlphaDark` in the dark appearance, smoked black at `clearPlateAlphaLight` in the light one, the material shows through) and the artwork a monochrome ink (near white in dark, near black in light) whose opacity is solved from the luminance of the artwork so that the colour on screen is affine in linear light between the glass and the ink (`clearBackdrop*` is the material the glass is assumed to sit on) and that never drops below `clearInkFloor` of the ink even for black artwork (a dark monochrome glyph such as Breeze's `network-wired`, `fcitx` or `zoom`, 1.1-1.4:1 on a dark panel in Default, stays about 2:1 against the plate). The same rule sets `darkArtLift` (Dark) and `tintedRampLowDark` (Tinted): measured with the model for a Breeze dark glyph (#232629): dark appearance 1.05-1.47:1 against the plate before, 2.0-2.2:1 now; light appearance 9-14:1 in Clear and Tinted, 2.2:1 in Dark. The price is internal contrast: black art against mid-grey art keeps about 1.6:1 in Clear (dark appearance) where Default has 5:1 (`tests/unit/tst_iconcontrast.qml`: a strong pair of 7:1 and more keeps 1.9:1, any pair from 4.5:1 up 1.5:1). Default has no plate, so a dark glyph keeps its 1.1-1.4:1 there: the theme's own limit (a dark Breeze variant in the dark appearance would be the fix, not a plate).
- `tinted` (macOS 26 Tinted): a dark tinted plate (`tintedPlateScale` of the tint's brightness) in the dark appearance, a light tinted plate (`tintedPlateMixLight`) in the light one; the luminance of the artwork is mapped onto a two-tone ramp of the tint (`tintedRampLow*` to `tintedRampHigh*`, one pair per appearance; a tile icon: the tint at the luminance of the pixel, see below). `icons.tint` is the tint (default: the accent colour).
**An icon that is already a rounded square is not put inside a second one (integrator decision 2026-10-23, the macOS model).** In Dark, Clear and Tinted an icon whose own shape is the system squircle (a tile: most icons of the composed `grassfield-icons`) is scaled so that its tile coincides with the plate and gets the mode's treatment as a whole; glyphs, logos without a tile and folders keep the plate and `sysGlyphScale`. Measured once per icon file on the alpha mask, see "Icon shader", "Own-tile rule".
**Small icons keep the Default artwork (integrator decision 2026-10-22, the macOS model: small icons stay legible).** The modes apply from `iconMode.minSize` (24 px) up. Below it (the surfaces that draw icons through `TintedIcon` below 24 px: the Files list rows and sidebar, 16 to 22 px, the Force Quit rows, 18 px, the Overview pill, 20 px, and the Dock's minimized-window badge, about 19 px, which is given its drawn size) every mode draws the Default artwork at its own size, with no plate and no effect item (`TintedIcon.modeActive` is false, `effectiveMode` is `default`; a folder's symbol, plate model and recolour layer follow it). At 16 px a plate and a recolour made json, cpp, txt, sh and csv the same tile and left obs, systemsettings and a red glyph unreadable in Dark, Clear and Tinted. A2 asserts it (rule D) and `tst_iconmodes` checks the switch at 16 and 24 px.
**Known issue (opt-in effect modes only, Default unaffected):** opening the Apps panel in Dark, Clear or Tinted has one render-bound slow frame per opening (the first draw of about 100 effects in the window, 11-16 ms of render time; per-opening frames over 25 ms: 5-8 against 0-2 in Default and in the earlier MultiEffect build). Tried without effect: keeping effects alive across hides, pacing their creation, an asynchronous Loader, a cached texture per icon (a hidden ShaderEffectSource frees its texture). The measurements of the last runs were taken while other sessions loaded the machine (Default itself at 73-79 % of frames within 16 ms), so the figure needs a quiet-machine run (`tests/visual/icon-modes-apps-perf.sh 3`). Since 2026-10-23 the own-tile shape reads (about a hundred icon files, on the GUI thread, each icon fading in when its read ended) no longer happen during the opening: `IconShapePrewarm` (hosted by the menu bar window, fed by `Apps.listedIconSources`) reads the files of the listed apps one at a time, `iconMode.prewarmGapMs` apart, in an effect mode, as soon as the list is known (audit PERF-01 / UX-13: Tinted 90 % of frames within 16 ms, target 95 %; to be re-measured with `tests/visual/icon-modes-apps-perf.sh 3 "default tinted"` on a quiet machine).

## Icon shader (system plate for every icon)
**One pass, no analysis (owner decision 2026-10-20).** `ui/Grassfield/Ui/shaders/icon.frag` (built to the committed `icon.frag.qsb` by `scripts/dev/build-shaders.sh`; `just check` runs `build-shaders.sh --check`, which fails when a `.frag` changed since its `.qsb` was built; the hash file `SOURCES.sha256` makes the check independent of the Qt version) draws, per fragment and for every icon in Dark, Clear and Tinted alike:
1. The system plate: a rounded square (`sysPlateInset` 0.04 of the box on each side, corner radius `sysPlateRadius` 0.225 of its side, the macOS squircle proportion): graphite in Dark (`darkPlateLuma`), glass in Clear (white at `clearPlateAlphaDark` in the dark appearance, smoked black at `clearPlateAlphaLight` in the light one, `clearPlateAlphaWallpaper` 0.7 over the bare wallpaper), tinted in Tinted (the tint at `tintedPlateScale` in dark, mixed `tintedPlateMixLight` to white in light).
2. The artwork: the icon texture scaled about the centre by `sysGlyphScale` 0.72 (an icon that is already a tile: by the scale that puts its tile on the plate, see the own-tile rule below) and recoloured by a fixed transform per mode that depends on the pixel alone (no neighbour, no analysis). Every map is increasing and works in linear light on the relative luminance `Y` of the pixel (gamma 2.2), so two parts of an icon that differ by a contrast ratio keep as much of it as the floor of the mode allows (black art mapped to a floor `lo` against white mapped to `hi` keeps `(hi + 0.05) / (lo + 0.05)` at most): Dark `d = mix(Y, c_lin, darkArtSaturation)`, `art = lift + (scale - lift) d` per channel in linear light (`lift` and `scale` are `darkArtLift` and `darkArtScale` as lightness, black stays darker than white art); Clear a flat ink (`clearInkDark` in the dark appearance, `clearInkLight` in the light one) with the opacity that makes `under + (ink - under) * Y` (dark; `1 - Y` for the smoked ink) the colour on screen, `under` being the plate over the assumed material, times the pixel's own opacity; Tinted `mix(tint * tintedRampLow, mix(tint, white, tintedRampHigh), Y)` in linear light with the pair of the appearance. The shader samples the texture once, unconditionally.
3. No rim (owner decision 2026-10-23: "Je vois que le changement de teinte ajoute un anneau au bord des icones... il devrait juste changer sa teinte... l'effet que tu fais avec les bordures rend ca moche."). The plate has no edge highlight in any mode, appearance or surface (the former `darkRim`, `clearRim`, `tintedRim`, `rimWidth`, `rimFullSize`, `rimMinWidth`, `rimMinOpacity`, `rimInner`, `darkRimLight` and `tintedRimMix/Shade` tokens and uniforms are gone): a mode only changes the colour of the icon; for an icon that is not a tile the plate stays, as the legacy-icon treatment of macOS, without an edge. A dark plate is therefore only as visible against a dark material as its own luminance and the A2 rule E allow (see the limits below).
**Own-tile rule (integrator decision 2026-10-23, the macOS model).** macOS puts a legacy icon on the system squircle only when it is not already that shape; an icon that is already a rounded square is shown at the plate's size and the mode's treatment is applied to the whole icon. The shader is unchanged: it already takes the artwork scale as a uniform (`sysGlyphScale`), `IconEffect.artScale` feeds it. `TintedIcon` decides the scale once per icon FILE: `IconShape` (singleton cache, keyed by the source string, LRU bound 1024) is filled by a `Canvas` read of the icon (the technique of `FolderPlate`: `loadImage`, then `createImageData(url)`: the image's OWN pixels, whatever its source (an `image://icon` pixmap, an SVG, a PNG) and the output's scale or the window's pixel ratio; `IconTile.sideOfImageData` samples its alpha down to `iconMode.ownTileProbeSize` 96 px, nearest, and measures that. Never `drawImage`: on a 1.25 output it drew an `image://icon` pixmap at half the requested size, so every icon read as "empty", not a tile, and sat as a coloured tile inside a pale plate (`tests/shell/icontile_hidpi.sh` runs the icon tests at 1.05, 1.25, 1.5 and 2); a claim so that at most `ownTileConcurrency` (8) files are read at once in the whole application and two icons never read the same file, a `ownTileTimeout` of 3 s gives up as a non-tile) and the pure function `IconTile.measure` runs on the alpha mask only (no colour analysis, nothing in the shader). An icon is a tile when: its box (the extent of the pixels with alpha >= `ownTileAlpha` 0.5 along the two middle rows and the two middle columns, so a shadow, which is below the threshold, and a badge sticking out of a corner do not count) is square (`ownTileSquare` 0.03), centred (`ownTileCentre` 0.03) and at least `ownTileMinSide` 0.7 of the icon box; its fill by opaque pixels is within `ownTileFillMin` 0.9 .. `ownTileFillMax` 0.998 (a radius-22.5-percent rounded square fills 0.957, a macOS squircle 0.95, a circle 0.785, a sharp square 1); at least `ownTileCornersEmpty` 3 of the 4 corners are empty `ownTileProbe` (0.01) inside the box (a sharp square has none, a badge may fill one); and the middle half of each of the 4 edges is filled for at least `ownTileEdge` 0.9 (a folder's tab and a glyph's arms leave gaps). The scale of a tile icon is plate side over tile side, `(1 - 2 * sysPlateInset) / side`, clamped to `ownTileScaleMin` 0.85 .. `ownTileScaleMax` 1.35 (`IconTile.artScale`): its own tile lands on the plate. Anything else (folders, glyphs, logos without a tile, circles, sharp squares, any file that could not be read) keeps `sysGlyphScale`. The plate stays under a tile icon, so it costs nothing: in Dark and Tinted the opaque tile covers the plate (the plate's corners lie inside a tile of equal or smaller radius), in Clear the glass shows through the translucent ink exactly as it does for a glyph. **No pop.** The scale is unknown until the file is read, so the effect of an icon whose file is not read yet is drawn at opacity 0 and fades in over `motion.fast` (`Appearance.duration`) once its scale is known: no icon is ever seen at one scale and then the other. A file already read is known at once (no fade, no read: Dock, Apps and Search share the cache). Default draws the plain image: `shapeWanted` is false, no `Canvas` and no read exist (`Loader.active` false, `IconShape.count` stays 0; `tst_icontile.qml`), and below `iconMode.minSize` (24 px) nothing is read and the Default artwork is drawn (rule D). The folder symbol follows the artwork scale (`FolderIcon` uses `icon.artScale`); a folder is not a tile (tab: not square or gaps in the top edge; measured on the pack: 446 of 447 `places` folder, user and directory icons are not tiles, the one tile is the server rack `network-server.svg`).
Measured on the composed `grassfield-icons` (MacTahoe, Pebble, Breeze layers; `IconShape` sweep over the 2973 distinct icon files of the apps directories, offscreen, 96 px): 2727 tiles (2166 at 0.875, 388 at 0.854, 66 at 0.859, 33 at 0.906 own fixtures and hicolor, the rest 0.84 .. 1.0) and 246 not (126 not square: circles, folders, glyphs with an emblem; 92 fill: circles, discs, pinwheels; 14 edge, 8 corner, 4 off centre, 2 too small); of the 65 icons of the desktop entries of this machine that resolve to a file, 53 are tiles and 12 are not (Discord, Google Contacts, Messages and Photos, Zoom, Aether, Tensaku, Cliamp (a sharp square), Disk Utility, Files, Preview, Nautilus (a folder): circles, glyphs and folders, none a misread tile; Files is a folder, Preview a landscape frame, Disk Utility a portrait drive). A rejected tile (a rounded square whose bounding shape is broken by something that is not a corner badge) falls back to the plate: it looks like today, never worse. Throughput: serial 10 ms per file (30 s for 2973 files, SVG rasterised at 96 px on the offscreen software renderer); 100 distinct icons in a grid settle in 541 ms at 4 concurrent reads, 374 ms at 12 (headless, `tst_icontile`-style scratch test; 8 is used); a second surface over the same files is known at once (0 ms). The first open of the Apps panel after a switch to an effect mode therefore fades its icons in over about half a second; later openings are as before.
The folder colour (`FolderIcon`) is applied by this shader to the artwork only (uniforms `folderTint` and the MultiEffect arithmetic), after the mode transform and before the plate is composited: the plate keeps its look. Over the bare wallpaper (desktop icons: `TintedIcon.overWallpaper`, styled for the wallpaper whatever the window appearance, like the labels) Clear uses the strongly smoked plate so white ink reads on a white wallpaper and the plate edge on a black one; Clear over a material at transparency 0.8 or more is drawn the same way (`wallpaperLikeTransparency`), except over an opaque fill (`TintedIcon.overAccent`). Over the accent fill of a selected row (Search, Force Quit: `TintedIcon.overAccent`) the plate and the ink are the opposite of the fill (the row's text colour `accentText` says whether the accent is bright or dark): Clear gets an opaque plate (`clearPlateAlphaAccent` 0.9), dark with light ink on a bright accent and light with dark ink on a dark one, Dark keeps its graphite plate on both (on a dark accent the plate is 2.8:1 against it), Tinted takes the dark tinted plate on a bright accent and the light tinted plate on a dark one; Clear is never drawn as over the wallpaper there.
Why no analysis: the earlier design estimated each icon's own plate (16 probes, clustering, a second plate colour, a corner test, a stroke rule) in one then two passes. A uniform plate is how macOS treats an icon that is not made for the mode, and it cannot misread anything.

**Without a rim, a tile is set off by its own luminance (integrator task 2026-10-23, the macOS model).** The suite measured the owner's decision (no ring) on the merged design and failed 455 cells (313 rule I, 99 E, 43 S, 60 accent row, the Files row and a black folder on black), not for want of an edge but because, once a tile fills the plate, the maps of the three modes were applied to the whole tile and they lose contrast: every offset in them (Dark `darkArtLift` 0.32, Clear's glass and ink floor, Tinted's ramp floors) is added to both the glyph and the tile body, so a 2.4:1 pair of an orange tile and its white glyph became 1.7-2.1:1, and a lifted or darkened tile moved towards the mid-grey of a wallpaper (rule E). ## Folder colours (owner request 2026-10-19)
Folder icons are recoloured by a step separate from the icon modes: `FolderIcon { source, size, folderColor?, folderGlyph? }` wraps a `TintedIcon` (modes unchanged) and, when a colour applies, recolours it: in an icon mode the icon shader maps the luminance of the artwork onto the colour after the mode and before the system plate (`TintedIcon.recolor`; the plate keeps its look, so a black folder keeps a visible plate on a black wallpaper), in default mode (and when the shader failed) a layer with one `FolderEffect` (`MultiEffect`, colorization 1 over the luminance of the theme's folder; tokens `folderIcon*`) does the same arithmetic. The colour is the folder's own (`folderColor`: `theme`, `accent`, `#rrggbb`, `""` = none) else the global `icons.folderColor` (`Appearance.folderColorSetting`); `theme` means the icon theme's own colour and creates no layer and no effect (`FolderStyle.recolors`). The optional `folderGlyph` is a `Text` over the effect, in a clipped box the size of the icon, at `folderIcon.glyphCenterY` with edge `glyphScale` x the icon: emoji opaque in their own colours, a plain symbol an engraved shade of the plate under it with >= 3:1 against the plate: the raw icon's pixels under the symbol are read once per icon file and size bucket and the plate of every mode, colour and appearance is computed from them with the arithmetic of the icon shader (its per-pixel recolouring, over the system plate) and of MultiEffect (`FolderPlate`, `FolderStyle.plateThrough`, `glyphShade`, `glyphMinContrast`; one engraving direction per icon mode; the symbol shows at once with an estimate and settles with a cross-fade; details and measurements in docs/specs/files.md); the symbol rule (one grapheme cluster, no control or bidi characters) is `FolderStyle.normalizeGlyph`, the same as the C++ side (one algorithm over generated data, `tests/fixtures/glyph-vectors.json`). `SwatchRow` puts its keyboard ring on the chosen swatch, else the first, as soon as it has the focus. `FolderStyle.js` is pure: validation (`theme|accent|#rrggbb`), precedence, folder icon names, the effect parameters from tokens, the parser of a folder's stored value, of `getfattr` output and of the per-user store (shell side, used by the desktop). The setting is `icons.folderColor` in the contract (schema `pattern`, defaults, `Store.js`, shell store, Files and Settings read it live); Settings > Appearance > Folder colour row (Theme, Accent, eight swatches). The per-folder storage, fallback and Customize Folder are specified in docs/specs/files.md. `tests/unit/tst_foldercolors.qml`.

Focus and input of a hidden or disabled control (decisions of the controls review, macOS model):
- **Focus rule.** A control that becomes hidden or disabled while it has the focus resigns it: `FocusGuard` (inside every control but Button) gives the focus to the next control that can take it, never to the enclosing scope. A `SpinBox` or `TextField` is the AppKit text field that resigns first responder: ending the edit commits it, exactly as on Tab or a click elsewhere (a spin box hidden mid-edit commits once, at the hide). Tab, Backtab and Escape are never swallowed, by any control in any state, so the keyboard is never trapped and Escape always reaches the dialog around it. `Button` is the exception by contract: a hidden or disabled Button keeps the focus and swallows only its activation keys (Space, Return, Enter, Select), so that Escape reaches the polkit dialog (R12); the other controls that still hold the focus (code can give it) swallow their activation keys, and a text field or spin box swallows every key but those three, on press and on release.
- **Accepted assistive-technology limit.** `SetCurrentValue` (a slider, spin box or scroll bar value) and `SetTextContents` (`setText(Value)`) can still write to a disabled or hidden control. Qt writes the property directly and QML cannot tell that write from the host's own binding update, so refusing it would break or stale bound values; it comes from the user's own assistive-technology client and crosses no privilege boundary, and the models validate their own submits (the polkit model guards its submit). What QML can refuse is refused: typing, `InsertText`, delete and replace (the text of a hidden or disabled field is read-only), keys, and the press, toggle, increase and decrease actions. Fully closing the two writes would need a C++ accessible-interface override, outside this QML-only module.

## Material blur follows transparency (GF-125)
Hyprland holds one blur size and one pass count for every surface (the `ext-background-effect-v1` region and the layer rules carry no strength), so the shell asks the compositor for the pair of the current effective transparency; it never fakes a blur. Mapping (`Appearance.blurSize(t)`, `blurPasses(t)`, tokens `material.blurSize{Min,Max}`, `blurPasses{Light,Full}`, `blurPassesFrom`):
- size = round(12 + 24 t) px (36 at t = 1); at the default 0.25 it is 18 with 3 passes (2 passes below transparency 0.15). The defaults are exactly what `config/hypr/grassfield/looknfeel.lua` ships (a test pins it), so a config reload at the default restores a right value.
- Reduce Transparency = t 0: no surface requests blur; the minimum is kept.
- Sent by `qs.platform` `Compositor` (`Minimized.blurCall`: Lua `hl.config({decoration={blur={size,passes}}})`) over the request socket, one connection per push, so that the answer is read: `ok`, or `error: ...: unknown config key '...'` (`Minimized.blurAnswer`); an error answer stops the pushes and the config's own 18 / 3 stays. At start, on every change (debounced by `motion.fast`) and after `configreloaded`. Captured on Hyprland 0.56.2 (`tests/compositor/blur_events.sh`, nested): a push emits no socket2 event, a config reload emits exactly one `configreloaded`, and the push that follows it emits none, so a reload is always a real one (no echo, no loop); Reduce Motion left on for 30 s causes one motion push and no blur push; an unknown key is answered with the error above and emits no event (there is no `notify` event to wait for). No polling, no plugin needed.
- Cost: more transparency = a wider blur = more GPU; the pass count drops by one for nearly opaque materials.
- Verification: offline `tst_tokens` (mapping, monotonic, default = config) and `tst_minimized` (request builder, `blurAnswer` on the captured answers); nested, bare: `tests/compositor/blur_events.sh` (stages A to D above; PASS on 0.56.2).

**Window edge per appearance (ADR-0039 step 6, C-4; ADR-0024 note 2026-11-03).** Hyprland draws one border colour pair for every window (`general.col.active_border`, `inactive_border`, `border_size` 1: one device pixel at 1.25). macOS 27's contact edge is a dark device pixel in both appearances, so the shell sends the pair of the appearance it shows, the same way as the blur and on the same socket: `Minimized.edgeCall(colour, active, inactive)` (Lua `hl.config({general={col={active_border, inactive_border}}})`, `rgba(rrggbbaa)`) from tokens `window.edge{Dark,Light}` and `window.edge{Active,Inactive}Opacity{Dark,Light}` (dark: black 0.58 / 0.52, the config's own; light: black 0.125 / 0.11; the same darkness per point as macOS 27's half-point line); at start, on a change of appearance (debounced) and after a config reload, which restores the config's dark pair; an error answer stops these pushes until the next change. The dark appearance's light inner rim is not drawn: Hyprland 0.56.2's inner glow has no window rule, skips the rounded corners and lit the client's top row under a third-party titlebar, so the rim is left to the plugin (with its bar; ADR-0024 note 2026-11-03). Verification: `tst_minimized` (the call per appearance, the config's pair is the dark one and it sets no glow, the wiring read from `Compositor.qml`); nested: `tests/compositor/window_edge.sh` (the edge, no rim and the shadow measured on GF-REF captures at 1.25, with and without the plugin).
