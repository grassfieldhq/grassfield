# menubar spec · shell-engineer · M1 (T1/T2 global menus: M3, see menu-bridge.md)

## macOS reference behavior (from design/reference/, described in words — no Apple images committed)
Not yet measured (F1 pending). Assumed: 24 px translucent bar across the top; Grassfield mark (system menu) and bold active-application name (application menu) on the left; status items, Quick Settings icon and clock on the right. Menus open under their title, highlight rows in the accent color, and clicking another title switches menus.

## Purpose and non-goals
Per-screen top bar with the system menu, the application menus (T0: app, File, Window; T1/T2: the application's own menus through grassfield-menu-bridge, `docs/specs/menu-bridge.md`, ADR-0008), status items (third-party, Now Playing, Search) and menus and the Quick Settings entry. Non-goals now: Services and Settings… items, Bluetooth/brightness items, notification center.

## Functional requirements
0. Material (owner decision 2026-10-18): the bar is transparent by default, like macOS 26/27. Setting `menubar.showBackground` (bool, default false; Settings > Menu Bar, "Show menu bar background") restores the frosted band; text and glyphs then follow the appearance. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast show the band too, as macOS does (opaque under the first, frosted under the second), and under Reduce Transparency the band of a display that is not the active one dims its content but stays opaque (ADR-0039 step 25). Insets (`menubar.insetLeft/insetRight`), title padding (`menubar.itemPadding`, 10 per side), status glyph padding (`menubar.statusItemPadding`, 8.5; also the clock's left padding) and 16-pt glyphs (`menubar.iconSize`) follow docs/audits/visual-tahoe-2026-10-25.md § Menu bar at rest (ADR-0024 lot 4c, C-16: titles 22.5 apart ink to ink, the app name at 55, status glyphs 19-21 apart). The open-title capsule floats 1 px inside the 24-px bar, fully rounded, and reaches `menubar.highlightOutset` (4) past a title, 14 around its text (titles and the clock; status glyphs keep their own width).
0a. Adaptive foreground (macOS model, owner decision 2026-10-19): without the background the foreground follows what is behind the bar, per output. `shell/desktop/Wallpaper.qml` reports the relative luminance (WCAG, linear) of the wallpaper strip under the bar to the `BarTone` singleton (`shell/services/BarTone.qml`, pure logic in `BarToneLogic.js`) when the wallpaper or the output changes, never per frame: the procedural artwork is a known gradient (nothing is sampled), an image (the dev override `VisualFixtures.wallpaperFile`, the same path a user wallpaper will take) is drawn onto a small canvas (`menubar.toneSampleColumns` wide, the strip `menubar.toneSampleRows` tall) and averaged. Tone `light` (white text and glyphs, `colorDark.barText`, over a dark wallpaper; also the answer before anything is measured) or `dark` (black, `colorLight.barText`, over a light one). Hysteresis: dark needs a luminance above `menubar.toneDarkAbove` (0.18), light below `menubar.toneLightBelow` (0.175), in between the previous tone stays (first measurement: the middle). The band is as wide as gate A2 allows: white text keeps 4.5:1 up to 0.18 and black text from 0.175. Window content never matters (windows sit below the bar, the bar is over the wallpaper). The tone drives the titles, status glyphs, clock, highlight capsule (white or black hover tint) and the open-title capsule; the shadow is a single faint pass (`menubar.textShadowOpacity`), dark under light text and light under dark text, only while the bar has no background. `qs ipc call bartone status` prints `{output: {tone, luminance}}` (read-only test hook). Gate A2: roles "menu bar text and glyphs, light/dark foreground" (backdrop `bar-light-tone`/`bar-dark-tone`: the wallpaper itself, for the wallpaper colours each tone can be chosen for) in `tests/visual/contrast-roles.json`, exact calculation in `tests/visual/test_contrast_tokens.py`, rendered measure with `tests/visual/contrast.sh --surfaces desktop` (menu bar app name, clock, status icons over white, black, grey, noise, checker: 5.1:1 or more; white/black wallpapers 20:1).
1. One layer-shell window per screen, namespace `grassfield-menubar`, layer Top (Overlay while a full-screen window is on its output, req. 2b), exclusive zone 24 px (0 when the setting hides the bar on the desktop, req. 2b). Keyboard focus always None: hovering the bar or starting the shell never takes the keyboard from the focused window, and while a menu is open its popup holds the keyboard (Exclusive, the overlay). An OnDemand bar used to take the keyboard as a menu closed with the pointer resting on the bar, leaving the window Hyprland still counted active without its keys (`tests/compositor/click_focus.sh`). CTRL+F2 opens the system menu, whose popup takes exclusive keyboard focus.
2. Popups are drawn by `qs.overlay` (one full-screen layer-shell window per screen, mapped only while a popup is open, exclusive keyboard focus, click outside closes). State: `qs.services` `Popups` (kind, anchor, payload). While the overlay holds the keyboard (Exclusive), Hyprland 0.56.2 sends the pointer to it even over the bar strip (measured: a press on the Window title landed in the overlay at (175, 12) although its input mask left the strip out), so the overlay takes the whole output as input and hands the strip to that output's bar (`Popups.bars`, `trackAt(x)` / `pressAt(x)`).
0b. The band's glass (ADR-0024 point 3, integrator decision under the owner's delegation 2026-10-29): the band is the "menu" glass class, and its tone follows the wallpaper strip under it, not the appearance. When the strip statistics are known (a picture or a video) and the strip is what lies behind the bar (`BarHide.bandOnWallpaper`: the bar reserves space, so no window is under it, and no full-screen window is on the output), `BarTone.ranges[output]` holds the strip's p5 to p95 and `Appearance.barBand` takes the bar's own text tone (`BarTone.bandDarkOf`): dark glass with light text over a dark strip, light glass with dark text over a light one, in both appearances. The tint alpha (`GlassTint.bandAlpha`) is the class target raised to the smallest value that keeps the primary text (the bar has no secondary text) legible in every state the band shows, over each end of the strip and the colours that bound it: at rest 4.5:1 plus `Tokens.glassContrastMargin`, under the open title's capsule at its real fill (hover opacity x active factor = 0.18, in the band's hover colour) the same, and dimmed on a display that is not the active one (band and text at `menubar.inactiveOpacity`) 3:1. Text, disabled text, hover tint and tint colour on the band follow the band's tone. Reduce Transparency, an unknown strip (artwork, colour), a bar that overlaps windows (`always` and `desktop`) and a full-screen window keep the appearance's own colours and `Appearance.glassAlpha` (the worst case when a window may lie behind). Numbers (`tests/unit/tst_glasstint.qml`, tint alpha, A2 at rest / capsule / dimmed): the owner's video strip (p5 0.0528, p95 0.35) dark glass 0.64 at transparency 0.49 (7.73 / 4.73 / 3.06) and 0.65 at 0.25; before, the unclassed material gave 0.82 at 0.49 (`Appearance.tintAlpha` clamp) and the appearance's own light glass 0.79. `wallpaper-state.json` carries a `bar` block, `{output: true}` for the outputs whose band is on because of the wallpaper (`BarTone.forcedOutputs`, `BarTone.assists`; rewritten when it changes, atomically); Settings > Menu Bar reads it and, with the switch off, says "The wallpaper is too busy under the menu bar for its text to be read, so a background is shown." Known limit: a floating window dragged under a bar that reserves space (`never`, `fullscreen`) is still judged on the wallpaper's strip, about 2.5:1 for a white window over a dark strip at transparency 0.49. macOS never lets a window go under the bar, so the follow-up is one of two: keep windows out of the bar's zone, or drop the range (the worst case) while a window crosses the strip. Tests: `tst_glasstint` (palette, tone, every state, floor with the band's palette, Reduce Transparency), `tst_barhide` (`bandOnWallpaper`), `tst_bartone` (`forcedOutputs`), `tst_barbandwiring` (what the bar and the status writer must call).
2b. Several displays (macOS): the bar of an output that is not the focused one (`Compositor.focusedScreenName`) is dimmed, band and content at `menubar.inactiveOpacity` (0.65, `motion.fast`); a single output is never dimmed. The dimmed foreground still holds 3:1 against the wallpaper tone it was chosen for (two roles in contrast-roles.json; 0.5 measured 1.2 to 2.6).
2a. Menu tracking (macOS): a bar item acts on the press (a menu opens on mouse down); with one of the bar's menus open, moving onto another title or status menu item (system, app, the app's own menus, File, Window, network, volume, battery, Quick Settings) opens its menu without a click; a click on the open title closes it, on another title opens that one, on the clock, the Do Not Disturb item or empty bar closes the open menu first (then the clock opens Notifications). Left/Right in an open menu move to the previous/next title's menu, wrapping (Right on a row without a submenu, also inside a submenu; Left in the top-level menu; `MenuNav` actions `previousMenu`/`nextMenu`, `PopupMenu.menuStepRequested`, `Popups.stepMenu`). The cycle covers the status items that open a menu too (third-party tray items, Now Playing, network, sound, battery, Quick Settings; `MenuBar.navEntries`, bar order, wrapping); Search, the clock and Do Not Disturb are not menus and keep their own keys (SUPER+SPACE, SUPER+N). The panels hand Left/Right to the cycle when no control of theirs uses them (the sound menu starts on its mute button and keeps Up/Down for the volume; Quick Settings moves to the control beside first and steps at the edge of its grid). Keyboard access like macOS: CTRL+F2 opens the Grassfield menu (`menubar open system`), CTRL+F8 opens the first status menu (`menubar open status`, bind it in `bindings.lua`), then Left/Right walk the whole bar (`tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh` `keyboard-walk`, `keyboard-status`). No hover highlight in the bar: the capsule shows only while an item is pressed or its menu is open. Scenario: `tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh` (`openKind` IPC test hook).
2b. Auto-hide and full screen (macOS 26/27). Setting `menubar.autoHide` (Settings > Menu Bar, "Automatically hide and show the menu bar"): `always` (hidden on the desktop and in full screen), `desktop` ("On Desktop Only": hidden on the desktop, shown over full-screen windows), `fullscreen` ("In Full Screen Only", default: shown on the desktop, hidden in full screen), `never` (always shown, over full-screen windows too). Policy in `shell/menubar/BarHide.js`; the per-output state machine (shown, hiding, hidden, revealing, with a dwell at the edge) is the Dock's `shell/dock/AutoHide.js`, driven by a `FrameAnimation` that runs only while something changes (no frame loop at rest). The bar window never changes size: its content slides up out of view (`Tokens.menubarHideDuration`/`menubarRevealDuration`, Reduce Motion: no slide, shown at once) and only a `menubarRevealZone` (2 px) strip at the top edge takes input while it is away. It comes back when the pointer stays at the edge for `menubarRevealDwell`, or at once when a menu opens (CTRL+F2, IPC), and leaves `menubarHideGrace` after the pointer left the bar with none of its menus (nor Notification Center) open. Exclusive zone: only the modes that show the bar on the desktop, `fullscreen` and `never`, reserve the 24 px, constantly; `always` and `desktop` reserve nothing, so a maximized window is never resized by a reveal or a hide (lesson of NextKDE's auto-hide controller, ideas only). Over full-screen windows: Hyprland 0.56.2 draws a full-screen window above the Top layer (the bar stayed hidden under it), so while a window of the output's shown workspace is full screen (`Compositor.fullscreenOn`, Hyprland fullscreen mode 2, refreshed on the `fullscreen`, `workspacev2`, `openwindow`, `closewindow` and `movewindowv2` events) the bar window sits on the Overlay layer (above that window; the popup overlay is mapped later and stays above the bar) and draws the frosted band (the bar is over the window's content, not the wallpaper its tone was measured on; dark or light text follows the appearance). Keyboard focus stays None, the full-screen window keeps its size and its keys. A Split View Desktop (tiling step 6a, `docs/specs/tiling.md` TL33) counts as full screen for the bar (`Compositor.splitShown`): its windows take the whole output, the bar hides there and shows at its edge in the full-screen modes, and the Dock auto-hides on that output too. Test hook: `menubar autohide <output>` (JSON: mode, fullscreen, hides, state, visible, away, overlay, zone). Tests: `tests/unit/tst_barhide.qml`, `tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh` (`fullscreen-reveal`). At the session's start (ADR-0040 step 6, ID-5, ID-6, ID-20) the bar's content is final once the settings are read (`Entrance.settingsFinal`: `Settings.loaded`, or `session.settingsWaitMs`), its auto-hide machine then starts where it rests (`AutoHide.initial`, as the Dock's: an auto-hidden bar is never shown and slid away), and the band and content fade in with the Dock and the desktop icons (`Entrance.revealed`, `session.revealMs`).
2c. Press, drag, release (macOS): the button that opens a menu is still down, so the pointer stays with the surface it was pressed on (the bar, or the overlay's strip over the bar while another menu is open). That surface reports the pointer (`Popups.trackPointer`) and the release (`Popups.releasePointer`) in output coordinates; the overlay relays them to the open `PopupMenu` (`rowAt`, `trackPointer`, `releasePointer`, `containsPoint`, window coordinates, also across open submenus): the row under the pointer highlights like a hover (a submenu row opens its submenu), the release runs that row; a release on no row, on a disabled row, on a separator or back on the title leaves the menu open. Measured in the nested session: the pointer does NOT stay with the bar once the popup overlay maps (Hyprland 0.56.2 hands it to the new full-output layer and releases the held button 15 ms after the press; the real release then reaches no surface). So while the button of a bar press is down (`Popups.pointerHeld`, set by `BarButton` before it opens the menu, cleared by the bar's `PointHandler` on the release) the overlay maps with an empty input mask and without the keyboard; on the release it takes the whole output and the keyboard as before. `tests/visual/menubar-fullscreen.sh` (captures dark and light: away, mid-slide, shown; Reduce Motion) and `tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh` (`fullscreen-hidden`) cover req. 2b. Tests: `tests/unit/tst_popupmenu.qml` (relay), `tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh` (`press-drag-release`).
3. Grassfield menu: Recent Items ▸ (macOS Apple menu, 2026-10-22: `shell/services/RecentItems.qml`, pure logic `RecentItemsLogic.js`, tests `tests/unit/tst_recentitems.qml`): an "Applications" section with the last 10 applications the shell launched (every `Apps.launch` of an entry: Dock, Apps, Search, menus; launches from a terminal are not seen) and a "Documents" section with the last 10 recently used documents (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/recently-used.xbel`, read only, the same parser and root filter as Search: `GRASSFIELD_SEARCH_ROOT` or `$HOME`), then Clear Menu, which empties the applications and hides the documents used before it without writing recently-used.xbel (other applications share it); state `recent-items.json` (0600) in the Grassfield state folder. Choosing an application launches it, a document opens with its default application (`xdg-open`, the dev open policy in a dev session). About This Computer (Settings' own window since ADR-0040 step 1b: `grassfield-settings --about`, docs/specs/settings.md; an open one comes forward, `SettingsApp.openAbout`, found by its role "info" in Hyprland's window data), Settings… (launches `grassfield-settings`; `GRASSFIELD_SETTINGS_CMD` overrides the command in dev), Force Quit… (lists toplevels; the selected app's PID comes from Hyprland window data; `kill -9` only after a second confirmation), Sleep (`systemctl suspend`), Restart…, Shut Down… (confirmation, then `systemctl reboot`/`poweroff`), Lock Screen (PLACEHOLDER, disabled "(coming in M2)"), Log Out <user>… (confirmation, then `hl.dsp.exit()` through the platform layer; Quickshell wraps the request as `hl.dispatch(<request>)` when `Hyprland.usingLua`).
4. Dev guard: with `GRASSFIELD_DEV=1` Sleep, Restart and Shut Down never run `systemctl`; they log a warning and show a notice dialog. Log Out ends only the compositor of the session (the nested one).
5. Application menu (T0, real items only). The bar shows the focused app name (bold) followed by `File` (only when it has real items) and `Window`. The app is identified by foreign-toplevel app id mapped to a desktop entry, never by title. Models are pure (`shell/menubar/model/AppMenuModel.qml`, tested by `tests/unit/tst_appmenumodel.qml`); `AppMenus` (singleton) builds the live context and runs every item through the platform layer. Popup kinds: `app`, `appfile`, `appwindow`.
   - App menu: About <App> (desktop entry name, comment, generic name, id, command; the desktop-entry Version key is not exposed by Quickshell), Hide <App> (moves its windows to `special:hidden`; Dock click or Show All brings them back), Hide Others, Show All (enabled only when something is hidden), Quit <App> (xdg close request for every window of the app; not a kill).
   - File: New Window is shown only when the entry declares a `new-window` desktop action (that action runs), then every other `.desktop` Action as an item. Known limitation: without that action nothing is shown, because launching the entry again is not known to open a new window (`SingleMainWindow=true`, single-instance apps re-activate the existing one) and Quickshell does not expose `SingleMainWindow`. No real item: no File menu.
   - Window: Minimize, Zoom (toggles the maximized state), Enter/Exit Full Screen (toggle through the foreign-toplevel `maximized`/`fullscreen` requests), Move to Desktop > (existing Spaces, the current one checked and disabled, then New Desktop; moves without following), Bring All to Front, then the app's windows (title as plain text, elided to 40 characters, check on the focused one; a minimized window is marked and restored when chosen).
   - Shortcut text comes from one table in `AppMenuModel.qml` that mirrors `config/hypr/grassfield/bindings.lua` (SUPER+H Hide, SUPER+M Minimize, SUPER+ALT+F Zoom, SUPER+CTRL+F Full Screen). Update both together.
   - With a popup open the keyboard focus leaves the app, so the "focused window" of an app is its most recently focused window.
   - T1/T2 (M3): when the shown app exports its menus (Qt/KDE, Chromium; `shell/platform/MenuBridge`), its visible top-level menus follow the app name in its order and open as popup kind `appbridge` (`shell/menubar/BridgeMenu.qml`, routed by the overlay); the T0 File menu gives way (its desktop actions stay in the Dock menu), the T0 app menu and Window stay. Items show the app's labels (plain text), shortcuts, check marks, disabled state and submenus, and choosing one runs the app's own action. Without such a menu the T0 menus stay. Details: `docs/specs/menu-bridge.md` MB11–MB12.
6. Status area, real backends only, from the left (macOS order from the right: clock, Quick Settings, system items, third-party items): third-party status items, Now Playing, Do Not Disturb, Network (Quickshell.Networking: wifi strength icon, wired icon, off icon; menu: Wi-Fi on/off, current network), Volume (Pipewire default sink: icon follows level/mute, scroll ±5 %, menu: volume slider, mute, output device list that selects the default sink), Battery (UPower, hidden when no laptop battery; menu: percentage, time remaining/until full), Search, Quick Settings icon (see controlcenter.md), clock.
   - Third-party status items (macOS menu extras = StatusNotifierItem, `shell/menubar/TrayHost.qml` over Quickshell.Services.SystemTray, `TrayButton.qml`): the application's own icon at `menubar.iconSize`, one slot per item object (service and path), never per id (Chromium and Electron register several items with one id, and a Passive item, an item with no icon and a dead item get no slot, so no phantom slots). Press: with ItemIsMenu the application's menu, otherwise Activate (the SNI semantics; the item's menu is its context menu); right press: the menu; middle press: SecondaryActivate; wheel: Scroll (vertical and horizontal); NeedsAttention draws a dot. The menu is the application's DBusMenu drawn with the bar's PopupMenu in the overlay (popup kind `tray`, `TrayMenu.qml`, rows from `TrayMenuNode.qml`; check marks, disabled rows, separators and submenus; choosing a row sends the DBusMenu click) and a tray item with a menu joins the hover tracking of 2a. The title (else the tooltip title, else the id) is the accessible name. Pure decisions in `StatusLogic.js` (`tests/unit/tst_statuslogic.qml`). The host never starts in a dev session on the owner's session bus (it would try to own `org.kde.StatusNotifierWatcher`): only with GRASSFIELD_PRIVATE_BUS=1 or outside dev.
   - Now Playing (macOS Control Center, Now Playing "Show When Active"): a glyph while an MPRIS player has a session (playing, or paused with a track loaded) and `menubar.showNowPlaying` (default true; Settings > Menu Bar). Its popover (popup kind `nowplaying`) is the Quick Settings Now Playing module (artwork, title, artist, previous, play/pause, next) driven by the `Media` service. On the nested session's private bus `Media` also accepts a test player and its transport is live there (the owner's players are not on that bus).
   - Settings > Menu Bar (2026-10-25): the status items and the clock are configurable (`menubar.showWifi`, `showSound`, `showBattery`, `showBatteryPercentage`, `showFocus`, `showNowPlaying`, `showSearch`, `showScreenCapture` (off by default: the Screen Capture item, which opens the capture toolbar on its display), `showTrayItems`, `clockDate`, `clockShowDayOfWeek`, `clockHours`, `clockShowAmPm`, `clockShowSeconds`, `clockFlashSeparators`, `recentItems`; `docs/specs/settings.md`). A hidden item takes no slot, is skipped by the keyboard cycle and counts for nothing in the crowding width. The clock uses `ClockFormat.js` and ticks every second only with seconds on. Day and month follow the region's order (`ClockFormat.monthFirst` on the locale's short date format: "Mon Oct 26" for en-US, "Mon 26 Oct" for en-GB and fr; `orderDate` swaps the translated pattern), and the date and the time are two spaces apart (macOS 9.5 against a 6-pt word gap).
   - Clicking the Now Playing artwork or title (popover, Quick Settings module, widget) opens the app that plays (macOS; owner report 2026-10-25; a locked session does nothing, the windows are the ones the Dock groups under the entry, and from an overlay panel the focus waits for the overlay to unmap, like the Dock menu): `Media.openPlayerApp()`, plan in `shell/services/MediaLogic.js` (`tst_medialogic.qml`): all of the app's windows minimized, bring the most recent back; else the player's MPRIS `Raise()` when `canRaise`; else the app's most recent window; else launch the desktop entry named by `desktopEntry` (or the identity). In a dev session Raise is suppressed (the transport guard). The popover or panel closes.
   - Input menu (macOS: the input source's abbreviation in the menu bar, shown as soon as there are two or more input sources; `shell/platform/InputSource.qml`, popup kind `input`, `shell/overlay/InputMenu.qml`): the source in use as its code in capitals ("FR", "US"; a second variant of one layout adds it, "FR bepo"), left of Wi-Fi. Its menu lists the sources by their xkb names (`base.lst`), shown in the shell's language (`layout-names.py` reads xkeyboard-config's translation, in one run for all the rows; English until it answers, and always for matching the source in use) with a check on the one in use, a separator, Show Emoji & Symbols (the Emoji & Symbols panel, ADR-0045 OC-3) and Open Keyboard Settings… (Settings > Keyboard). A choice runs `switchxkblayout all <n>` on Hyprland's request socket (`n` is the source's own index in Hyprland's list, which a layout name that is not an xkb code does not shift) (a request, not a dispatcher; `all`, so a keyboard plugged in later and a second keyboard stay together). The list is `j/devices` of the main typed keyboard (a button or a virtual keyboard is not one), read at start, on `configreloaded` and when a layout event arrives with an empty list; the source in use follows `activelayout` (xkb's name; a variant's name that `base.lst` words differently is matched on the words before its parenthesis). Layout events of a virtual keyboard are ignored like its entry in the list. Never polled. Not shown with one source, without a compositor, or when `keyboard.showInputMenu` is off (Settings > Keyboard > Input Sources, "Show Input menu in menu bar", default on). Keys (`config/hypr/grassfield/bindings.lua`, bound only from two layouts, `locked` so the lock screen has them): Ctrl+Space selects the previous source and Ctrl+Option+Space the next one, on every keyboard (`hyprctl switchxkblayout all prev|next`). Known difference from macOS: "previous" is positional (the source before this one in the list), where macOS Ctrl+Space returns to the most recently used source; the two differ only with three sources or more. Tests: `tests/unit/tst_inputsource.qml`.
   - Search (macOS shows the Spotlight magnifier by default): a magnifier that toggles Search, lit while Search is open, hidden by `menubar.showSearch` (default true; same Settings group). It is not a menu: it does not take part in hover tracking.
   - Screen recording (ADR-0045 OC-1; docs/specs/system-dialogs.md "Screen recording" SC15): while `grassfield-capture`
     records (its state through `CaptureState`), a stop item left of the screen-sharing indicator: the `record-stop` glyph and
     the elapsed time (m:ss, h:mm:ss from an hour, tabular figures). A click stops the recording (the helper's `stop`, as
     Ctrl-Super-Esc); it opens no menu and is not in the menu cycle. Never hidden by crowding (as the screen-sharing indicator).
     Known limitation: unlike the screen-sharing indicator it is not pinned in the corner when the bar hides over a full-screen
     window; it comes back with the bar.
   - Ongoing activities ("In Progress", ADR-0052; host: docs/specs/menu-bridge.md MB18): one item per activity an application
     published through `org.grassfield.Ongoing1`, right of the third-party items, at most `ongoing.barMax` (3, the most recently
     started). The item is the kind's heavy glyph (`clock`, `transfer`, `record`, `download`, `phone`, `activity`) and, when there
     is one, its time (a countdown rounded up, or the elapsed time, m:ss and h:mm:ss) or its percent, in tabular figures; the
     time moves every `ongoing.tickMs`. A click opens popup kind `ongoing` (`shell/overlay/OngoingPopover.qml`, `ongoing.popoverWidth`
     wide, the menu glass): the app's icon and name from its verified desktop entry, the title and subtitle as plain text, a bar
     (or the sweep) or the time, and the app's buttons (at most 3; a click sends the action and closes). The items are in the
     menu cycle and menu tracking. While the session is locked every item and the popover show "In Progress", the glyph and the
     progress only, and buttons do nothing. Optional for crowding, as Now Playing. Model: `shell/platform/Ongoing.js`
     (`tests/unit/tst_ongoing.qml`). Rendering is NOT VERIFIED in the container that built it (no Quickshell runtime).
   - Crowding (macOS: status items that would collide with the menus are hidden): the status area gets the width between the application menus and the right inset; third-party items, Now Playing and Search (the optional ones) are hidden leftmost first until the rest fits; the system items, Quick Settings and the clock stay, and an application name too long for the room they leave is elided, so nothing overlaps the menus. Pure decision `StatusLogic.hiddenItems` (`tst_statuslogic.qml`); test hook `menubar statusState`.
7. Keyboard in menus: Up/Down/Home/End, Return/Space activate, Right opens a submenu, Left closes it, Esc closes. Disabled rows are skipped.
8. Every string uses `qsTr()`; every control has `Accessible.name`/role and a visible focus ring.

## Acceptance criteria
- Screenshots of the system menu, app menu, volume menu and an app's About panel (L2; About This Computer is Settings' window, docs/specs/settings.md). `qmllint.sh` 0 warnings (L0). `tests/unit/tst_menunav.qml`, `tst_popupmenu.qml` (L0).
- Dev guard: Shut Down confirmation in the nested session logs `suppressed systemctl poweroff` and shows the notice (L2).

## Interfaces / contracts (versions)
Reads `Compositor` (toplevels, focused app, pid, hide, close, exitSession), `MenuBridge` (T1/T2 menus, menu/1), `Settings`, `Appearance`, `Audio`, `Connectivity`, `Power`, `Popups`, `Media`, `TrayHost`. Test hooks (IpcHandler): `menubar open <system|app|appfile|appwindow|nowplaying|...> <output>`, `menubar panel forcequit <output>` (Popups refuses a kind its Overlay does not draw, `shell/services/PopupKinds.js`), `menubar close`, `menubar bridgeState <output>` (JSON: shown window, bridge state, left titles and open bridge menu rows in output coordinates), `menubar statusState <output>` (JSON: right edge of the application menus and the visible status buttons, name and x, w), `controlcenter <transparency|appearance|reduceTransparency|reduceMotion>`.

`windows` IPC (platform, for key bindings): `windows minimize` (focused window), `windows hide` (focused app).

## Dependencies (verified versions)
Quickshell 0.3.1: PanelWindow, WlrLayershell, BackgroundEffect, SystemClock, DesktopEntries, UPower, Pipewire, Networking, Mpris, Services.SystemTray (StatusNotifierItem host), QsMenuOpener (DBusMenu), Io (Process, FileView), IpcHandler. Hyprland IPC only inside `shell/platform`.

## Degraded behavior
No battery: item hidden. NetworkManager absent: network menu says so. No PipeWire sink: slider shows 0 and does nothing. No `lspci`: graphics line omitted. Unknown PID: Force Quit disabled.

## Automated tests · Manual verification steps
`scripts/dev/test.sh`. Manual: nested-up, open foot, `qs ipc call menubar open system GF-REF`, `shot.sh`; keyboard flows with `wtype -k Down -k Return` on the nested socket.

## Performance · Accessibility · i18n
Event-driven (no polling). Menus have `Accessible.role: PopupMenu`/`MenuItem`. Strings via `qsTr()`. Clock date pattern is translatable.

## Known limitations
- Gap audit against macOS 26/27 and NextKDE (2026-10-22, integrator; `tests/visual/menubar-macos.sh`): fixed — open on press, hover tracking across titles and status items, Left/Right between menus, no idle hover highlight, a click on another title while a menu is open (it used to only close the menu), press-drag-release, the bar over full-screen windows and the "Automatically hide and show the menu bar" setting (req. 2b, 2c). Still missing: third-party status items (StatusNotifierItem tray: no host at all, so Steam, Discord, Telegram, nm-applet show nothing), the Search (Spotlight-like) status icon, Recent Items in the Grassfield menu, a T0 Edit and Help menu for apps that export no menus (when the Edit menu comes, it ends with Emoji & Symbols and its Ctrl+Super+Space, ADR-0045 OC-3; until then the panel has the key only, docs/specs/clipboard.md ES-10), status item reordering (macOS: Cmd+drag) and hiding, item overflow when app menus and status items do not fit (macOS hides status items that would collide).
- Status area (2026-10-22): third-party status items, Now Playing, Search and crowding are built. Not built: status item reordering (macOS: Cmd+drag) and hiding by the user, an overflow menu for hidden items (they just disappear while crowded), Bluetooth, Focus and Screen Mirroring-like system extras. A Passive tray item is never shown (no "always show" setting). Tray icons are the applications' own images, so the A2 contrast gate covers only the Grassfield glyphs (regions "menubar status icons" and roles "menu bar text and glyphs"); a coloured icon over a wallpaper is the application's choice. An item with ItemIsMenu false and a menu opens the menu on right press only (left press is Activate), so an app whose Activate does nothing looks dead on left press. Item icons are drawn as given (IconName, absolute path or pixmap through Quickshell); an item whose theme icon is missing shows nothing. `tests/integration/tray_host.sh` needs a private D-Bus daemon and skips outside an environment that can create Unix sockets (the Claude Code sandbox cannot); the nested checks are `tests/visual/menubar-status.sh`. In the nested session GF-REF sits at x=1440 and the overlay that maps under the pointer is told the pointer at x-1440 until it moves (a compositor quirk, harmless on a single output at x=0): `menubar-status.sh` moves 1 px before releasing. The Search status item was verified opening and closing Search; whether SUPER+Space and the item stay in step when Search is closed by Escape follows `Search.open` and was not tested separately.
- A click with no pointer motion at all since a menu opened reaches no surface: Hyprland gives a layer that maps under a resting pointer no pointer focus until the pointer moves (measured: the second click logged nowhere; 1 px of motion, then the click reached the overlay and closed the menu). A real mouse moves; a trackpad tap may not. A fix needs the compositor plugin to re-evaluate the pointer focus when the overlay maps.
- Adaptive foreground: a wallpaper whose strip luminance is 0.16 to 0.19 (for example a mid blue, #1d6fd8 is 0.166) gets about 4.3-4.4:1 whichever tone is chosen (the crossover of white and black text); the faint shadow helps there. One tone per bar: a wallpaper that is light on one side and dark on the other is averaged. The artwork, a colour and the dev image override report one mean luminance; a user picture (ADR-0016) reports its strip statistics and `BarTone.reportStrip` decides from the 5th and 95th percentile: the tone with the better worst-case contrast, with a margin (`menubar.toneMargin`) before it flips, and the frosted band on that output when neither reaches `menubar.toneNeededContrast` (4.5:1) until the wallpaper changes (`qs ipc call bartone status` reports `assist`, `white` and `black`; the shell logs `wallpaper tone=… worst=… assist=…`). The tone switches at the middle of the picture crossfade. Checked in the nested session with generated worst cases (`tests/visual/wallpaper-legibility.sh`: black, white, split, horizon, threshold, blocks, noise).
- Zoom and Full Screen use the foreign-toplevel requests; whether a client or Hyprland honors them was checked only for the menu wiring, not per app. Window menu list with several windows is covered by the unit test, the nested screenshot has one window.
- Move to Desktop lists the Desktops of the focused output only; SingleMainWindow is read per entry once (cached for the session).
- The Sleep/Restart/Shut Down paths were exercised only in the dev guard; the real `systemctl` calls are untested by design.
- Submenus are blurred two levels deep (the overlay's blur region nests `PopupMenu.submenuItem`); a third level is not.
- About "Version" of an app is unavailable. Menu bar items are not reachable with a global shortcut yet (needs a Hyprland binding calling IPC; config/ is not mine).
- Per-screen focus is global: every bar shows the globally focused app. While a popup holds keyboard focus the bar keeps showing the last app.
- Stages on, Hyprland has no active window (an Exclusive layer holds the keyboard after a swap, stages.md ST24): the bar names the front app in the last Stages state of the focused output (`front.app` of the `hyprctl grassfield-stages` reply that StagesLink fetches on each `stages` notice and hands to `Compositor.applyStagesState`: the event itself is only a notice; via `Apps.nameFor`) and shows its T0 menus (macOS: the frontmost app stays named behind a system modal). Display only: the Window menu's Minimize, Zoom, Full Screen and Move to Desktop are disabled (macOS greys window commands with no key window; Hide, Quit, Bring All to Front stay). It ends as soon as Hyprland reports an active window, Stages is off (`on:false`) or not active in the shell, the plugin is gone, the state is unknown (StagesLink gave up its fetch after three retries), or the front window no longer exists (Hyprland's window list). Never used with Stages off. The app text is treated as untrusted: control and bidi characters dropped, at most 128 characters, drawn as plain text. A popup open keeps the last-app rule above. Pure logic `shell/platform/StagesFront.js`, tests `tests/unit/tst_stagesfront.qml` and `tst_appmenumodel.qml`; nested check (not automated, 2026-10-24, Hyprland 0.56.2 efb50993): three stages, the Notification Center open (Exclusive), `qs ipc call stages activate 1`: `hyprctl activewindow` "Invalid", the bar names `gf-stage-b` with its Window menu (screenshot). Known gap: when the Notification Center then closes, FocusReturn asks for the window that was active when it opened (`remembered`), which brings the swapped-away stage back to the front.
- Wi-Fi network list/connect and Bluetooth are M2 (Quick Settings spec).
