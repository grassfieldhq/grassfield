Keyboard shortcuts and trackpad gestures reach the desktop directly: Overview and Apps now follow your fingers, and shortcuts answer faster.
The Dock, Overview, notifications and Search are smoother, with lighter shadows and animations that keep their momentum.
Grassfield’s window plugin now works with Hyprland 0.57 as well as 0.56, so an updated system keeps its full window controls.
The lock screen holds across sleep, idle and display-off, even when the desktop restarts, and follows the system’s lock request.
System updates and the Store refuse packages signed with an expired or revoked key, and a Store install survives a logout.
Memos never quits with unsaved text and tells you when it cannot save; renaming on the desktop never replaces another file.
Files no longer freezes on slow drives, and its window shortcuts wait for text fields and sheets.
The apps’ global menu is back for every app, and Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and network passwords are handled more reliably.
Displays in Settings never overlap or drift apart.
Diagnostic reports now include memory use, stopped services, the desktop’s own logs and your recent crashes.
