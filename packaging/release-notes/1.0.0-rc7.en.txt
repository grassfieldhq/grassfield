External displays that come back from sleep without a picture are switched on again by themselves, and waking from sleep lights the lock screen at once.
Choose the main display in Settings > Displays (Use as), or drag the menu bar to another display in Arrange; panels that are not high density offer their native resolution.
The menu bar is translucent on every display, and Settings > Wallpaper shows at once which displays a choice changes.
Screenshots are saved in Pictures > Screenshots and recordings in Videos; the capture shortcuts work on the numeric keypad, and the menu bar can show a Screen Capture item (Settings > Menu Bar).
Files: add folders to the sidebar (Add to Sidebar, or drag them in), Cut and paste with Ctrl+X, a steadier Columns view and narrower menus; items cut in Files can be pasted on the desktop.
In the Grassfield session, Preview and Files become the default apps for pictures, PDF documents and folders only where you had not chosen another app yourself; choose the app for a type in Get Info > Open with > Change All. The Omarchy session keeps its own defaults.
A window moved to a smaller display fits it, dragging a window to the top fills the screen at once, and windows no longer stay under the menu bar.
Apps that draw their own title bar show only one, and Chromium and Google Chrome show the window buttons with their GTK look (in Chrome, choose Settings > Appearance > Mode: GTK once).
Settings > Privacy > Screen Sharing now also shows, under Other Apps, screen sharing kept by apps the system cannot name, so you can remove it.
